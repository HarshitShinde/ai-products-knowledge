**Self-Study Crowdsource Activity 3.1: Applications of Neural Networks \[15 minutes\]**

**Learning Outcome Addressed**

* Evaluate the applicability of neural network architectures to various domains and problems.

*(Note: This is a self-study activity and does not count toward the completion of the program.)*

### ---

**Introduction**

Artificial neural networks (ANNs) have revolutionized various fields including computer vision, natural language processing, speech recognition, and healthcare. Understanding where and how specific neural network architectures (such as CNNs, RNNs, Transformers, and GANs) are deployed in real-world applications helps in selecting the right architecture for specific problem domains.

### ---

**Tasks**

1. **Research an Application:** Identify a real-world product, system, or case study that utilizes neural networks to solve a complex problem.  
2. **Identify Architecture & Domain:** In your post, specify:  
   * The application or product name.  
   * The underlying neural network architecture used (e.g., Convolutional Neural Networks for image tasks, Recurrent Neural Networks/Transformers for sequential data).  
   * The specific problem being addressed and the primary benefit delivered by the neural network approach.  
3. **Write Discussion Post:** Share a concise summary (100–150 words) of your findings in the discussion forum.  
4. **Peer Interaction:** Read the posts submitted by your peers and respond with thoughtful comments or questions to compare their chosen architecture with yours.

### ---

**Submission Instructions**

* **Suggested Word Count:** 100–150 words  
* **Average Completion Time:** 15 minutes
